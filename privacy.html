Privacy, in plain language

This page exists so you know what happens to what you share with me. Short version: very little, on purpose.

This website

The site itself sets no cookies, runs no analytics, collects nothing, and makes no third-party requests unless you choose to follow an external link.

If you email me

Mail to rita@lucentiaworks.com is routed by Cloudflare and delivered to a Gmail inbox I control. Treat it like ordinary business email.

If we work together

Discovery calls are recorded only with your OK, stated at the start of the call. What you share may be processed in professional AI tools and access-controlled storage I manage. I do not use your material to train public models. I delete working files on request where technically possible; backup copies expire on provider schedules. Before paid work begins, you will receive written terms explaining the tools, retention rules, and how to ask for deletion.

Your rights

Ask me what I hold about you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it: rita@lucentiaworks.com. If you are in the EU, these requests are honored under GDPR.
